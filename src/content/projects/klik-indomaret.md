## Overview

Klik Indomaret is the online shopping platform of Indomaret, an Indonesian retail chain. I worked on its backend as an engineer at SG-EDTS, on services that have to stay available and fast for a national retail audience.

## My role

- Designed and maintained microservices with a focus on high availability and performance.
- Built backend services and APIs, including gRPC-based microservices.
- Took part in system design, project structuring, and technical documentation.

## Stack

- **Services:** Java Spring Boot and Go, communicating over gRPC.
- **Messaging:** Kafka for real-time data exchange and decoupled communication between services.
- **Platform:** Google Cloud (Cloud SQL, GKE), Docker, and Kubernetes.

## Outcomes

- A microservices architecture maintained for high availability and performance.
- Established practices for scalability and maintainability across the services I worked on.
